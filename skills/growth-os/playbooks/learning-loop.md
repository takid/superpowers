# learning-loop playbook

## Current best

**Minimum sample before any conclusion:** 30 outcomes per arm for lead-facing variants, 10 posts per arm for content, 14 days maximum wait.

**Decision rule default:** test wins at +15% relative on the named metric. Kill at -30% after half the sample.

**Segment cuts, always:** postcode, service, client type, channel, day of week, hour of day.

**Rewrite discipline:** cite the number, keep `Current best` under 300 words, move losers to `Retired`, never delete `Learned`.

**Bottleneck selection:** largest gap to target by stage rate, not by absolute count. If two stages tie, pick the earlier one.

**Target raising:** any target beaten by 20%+ for a week is raised by 10% the same Monday.

**Monthly:** competitor-intel run, coverage map status, three-line strategy note.
