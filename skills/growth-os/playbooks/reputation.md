# reputation playbook

## Current best

**Ask timing (seed):** same day, 17:00-19:00, only on jobs with no issue logged.

**Review ask `rp-ask-v1` (seed, WhatsApp or text):**
> Hi [first name], thanks for having us today for the [service]. If you've got a minute, a Google review helps a small local team more than anything: [link]. If you can mention [area] and the [service], it helps neighbours find us. Coventry Cleans.

**Reminder `rp-ask-r1` (+3 days):**
> Hi [first name], no pressure at all, just the review link again in case it's useful: [link]. Thanks again. Coventry Cleans.

**4-5 star reply (seed):** first name, the service and area once, thanks, under 60 words, no pitch.

**3 star reply (seed):** "Thanks for the honest feedback, [first name]. That's not the standard we aim for in [area]. I'd like to hear what we missed; please call 07544 308764 and ask for Taka."

**1-2 star holding reply (seed, post immediately):**
> We're sorry to read this, [first name]. We'd like to put it right. Please call 07544 308764 and ask for Taka so we can sort it quickly.

**Never:** argue in public, name a cleaner, mention another client, offer money in a public reply.

## Live experiment

- Hypothesis: a WhatsApp ask gets more reviews than an email ask.
- Control: `rp-ask-v1` WhatsApp. Test: `rp-ask-v1e` same copy by email.
- Metric: reviewed / asked. Min sample: 30 per arm. Rule: +15% relative.
