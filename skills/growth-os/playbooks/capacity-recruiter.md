# capacity-recruiter playbook

## Current best

**Trigger (seed):** utilisation above 85% for 14 days, or any turned-away job. Otherwise do nothing.

**Bench target:** 2 trial-passed cleaners when utilisation is above 75%.

**Ad `cr-ad-v1` (seed):** title "Domestic and End of Tenancy Cleaner, Coventry (CV1-CV8)". Body: who we are (local, insured, standards-led), the work (named services and areas), hours, pay stated as a range Taka sets, what we look for (reliable, thorough, own transport preferred), how to apply (reply with name, area, availability, experience). No agency language.

**Screening score (1-5):** Coventry-based, right to work, transport for target postcodes, availability matching demand days, experience in the priority service. Score 4+ gets a call.

**Trial shift:** paid, 3 hours, with a senior cleaner, on a recurring domestic job not an end of tenancy. Senior gives a yes/no and one line.

**Cleaner referral bonus:** set by Taka, paid after the referred cleaner's first month.

## Live experiment

- Hypothesis: Facebook jobs posts bring more 4+ candidates than Indeed for this role.
- Control: Indeed. Test: Facebook jobs.
- Metric: candidates scoring 4+ per post. Min sample: 3 posts per channel. Rule: +15% relative.
