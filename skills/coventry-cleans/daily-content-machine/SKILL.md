# Daily Content Machine — Coventry Cleans

Two modes. Same brand voice. Same pricing source. Same publishing routes.

**Full Mode** — triggered by "run the content machine", "daily content", "run social today",
or no specific brief. Runs the intelligence scan, 10 ideas, copy, image prompts, publishing.

**Quick Mode** — triggered by a specific brief ("write a Facebook post about end of
tenancy", "create 3 posts for CV4", "write an ad for deep cleaning"). Skips the scan.

Detect the mode from the user's message. If in doubt, run Full Mode.

---

## Two files govern this skill. Read them before writing copy.

- **`skills/coventry-cleans/pricing.md`** — every price that appears in public. Never
  hardcode a price in a post. Read it, use it, and obey its rules.
- **`skills/coventry-cleans/publishing.md`** — how a post reaches a platform. Never invent
  a distribution method. Only two routes are live.

If either file is unavailable, write the copy and publish nothing. Say so.

---

## Pricing in copy — read this before every run

**Do not publish an hourly rate.** Per visit and per job only. An hourly rate invites the
comparison against a £12/hr cash cleaner, which is the one fight Coventry Cleans cannot
win. Full reasoning in `pricing.md`.

**Lead with the recurring price.** One-off second and higher, if shown at all.

**The standard-clean and office hourly rates are currently suspended** pending a cost
check. Until `pricing.md` shows a verified date at the top, write posts that carry the
service and the proof but no price. A post without a price still generates enquiries. A
post with a below-cost price generates below-cost jobs.

---

## Brand Voice (applies to both modes — always)

- Old-school professional. Direct, locally grounded, no fluff
- No emojis unless Instagram (max 2)
- Always include a Coventry postcode (rotate CV1 → CV2 → CV3 → CV4 → CV5 → CV6)
- Voice-search hooks: "Looking for a cleaner in CV4?" style openers
- CTA every post: call, WhatsApp, or link to site
- Never sound AI-generated. Vary sentence length. Write like a business owner who has done
  the work

---

## Mandatory Post Structure (both modes)

1. **Hook** — hyperlocal, service-specific, voice-search phrasing
2. **Body** — what we do, why it matters, who it is for
3. **Proof / Trust** — insured, vetted, trained, local team, standards that get checked
4. **Price** — only per `pricing.md`. Omit entirely if the service's price is suspended
5. **CTA** — call, WhatsApp, or site

## Service Priority (both modes)

Ordered by what the work is worth to the business, not by how easy it is to write about.
This order is set by `docs/ccos/` and differs from earlier versions of this skill, which
ranked recurring domestic last.

1. **Recurring domestic** — the asset. A fortnightly customer is worth roughly 26 visits a
   year. This is what the business is actually built on and it should be promoted hardest
2. **Landlord, agent, and commercial contracts** — highest leverage per sale, and these
   customers reclaim VAT, which protects margin as turnover grows
3. **End of tenancy** — the door into landlord and agent relationships
4. **Deep clean** — the entry product that converts to recurring
5. **After renovation and one-off specialist** — opportunistic

Every post for services 3 and 4 should carry a line that opens the door to 1 or 2. A deep
clean post mentions keeping it that way. An end of tenancy post mentions landlords with
several properties.

# FULL MODE

## Step 1 — Three-Way Scan

Run all three searches in parallel.

### Competitor Scan
Search: `Fitz2Kleen Coventry`, `Officlean Coventry`, `Coventry Warwickshire Cleaning
Company`, `Fantastic Services Coventry`, `"Coventry cleaning" Facebook posts`.

Capture per competitor: which service they are pushing, what hook they use, what they are
not saying (pricing, postcode, proof), where Coventry Cleans is visibly stronger.

### Trending Topics Scan
Search: `Coventry property news [today's date]`, `Renters Rights Act update`, `Coventry
landlord news`, `UK cleaning industry news [current month]`, `end of tenancy cleaning
demand`, `Great British Spring Clean [current year]`.

Capture: national campaigns running now, regulatory changes, seasonal signals (school
holidays, student let season, weather), property and cleaning social trends.

### Niche News Scan
Search: `property management Coventry`, `HMO compliance [current year]`, `Coventry office
return`, `Coventry business news`, `cleaning business tips [current month]`.

Capture: compliance updates, local Coventry business news, office and commercial signals.

---

## Step 2 — Cross-Reference Engine

Score every signal against:

1. Content pillars — Trusted / Thorough / Consistent
2. Service priority — recurring domestic and commercial score highest
3. Lead intent — does this attract someone ready to book?
4. Local relevance — can a CV postcode or neighbourhood be named?
5. Competitive gap — are competitors not using this angle?

Score 1–10. Top 10 become content ideas.

## Step 3 — Assign to 10 Formats

Every full run produces exactly 10 posts, one per format.

| # | Format | Primary Platform | Application |
|---|--------|------------------|-------------|
| 1 | Deep Dive | Facebook | Market breakdown or service education |
| 2 | Reel / Short Video | Instagram | 5 tips, before/after reveal, fast local proof |
| 3 | Carousel | LinkedIn | Compliance news, market data, landlord angle |
| 4 | Thread / List Post | Facebook / LinkedIn | Myth debunking with local data |
| 5 | Tutorial / How-To | Facebook / Instagram | What we check, how to prepare |
| 6 | Story Poll | Instagram / Facebook | Local opinion question, no selling |
| 7 | Case Study | LinkedIn / Facebook | Deposit saved, property turned around, client retained |
| 8 | Quick Tip | Facebook / Google Business | One sharp insight |
| 9 | Reaction / Trend | Facebook / Instagram | Respond to a local or national moment |
| 10 | Behind the Scenes | Instagram / Facebook | Team, van, kit, process |

**Spread the mix across the live channels**, weighted by the service priority above. LinkedIn
carries the landlord, agent and commercial angles; Facebook and Instagram carry domestic
recurring; Google Business carries the local search work.

## Step 4 — Write Copy

Follow Brand Voice, Post Structure, Platform Rules, and `pricing.md`.

For paid ad variants:
- **Headline** max 40 chars
- **Primary text** max 125 chars
- **Description** max 30 chars
- **CTA button** Get a Quote / Message Us / Book Now
- **Audience note** who to target and which postcodes
- **Budget suggestion** daily spend

---

## Step 5 — Generate Image Prompts

One ready-to-paste prompt per post.

**Base template:**
```
[Scene description]. Photorealistic. Bright natural lighting. Clean, professional
aesthetic. British home interior/exterior. No text overlay. High resolution.
[Format-specific style note].
```

**Visual style:**
- Before/after split: left dirty, right spotless. Clean dividing line, high contrast
- Team in action: cleaner in dark branded polo, kitchen or bathroom, warm natural light
- Property exterior: Coventry terrace or semi, clean driveway, golden hour
- Close-up proof: gleaming tap, spotless oven interior, fresh white bedding
- Trust signal: clipboard with checklist, kit laid out, gloved hands on surface

| Format | Image Style |
|--------|-------------|
| Deep Dive | Cleaner with clipboard, or split comparison |
| Reel | Before/after or action shot, motion |
| Carousel | Flat-lay of products or checklist |
| Thread / List | Bold close-up of one clean object |
| Tutorial | Hands-on task, step-by-step feel |
| Story Poll | High-contrast background, minimal |
| Case Study | Property exterior and interior split |
| Quick Tip | Single clean room, one focal point |
| Reaction / Trend | Cleaner casual but professional |
| Behind the Scenes | Team candid, van, kit |

# SHARED STEPS (both modes)

## Step A — Draft to file FIRST

Always save before attempting to publish, so a failed route never loses the work.

**Full run:** complete output to
`coventry_cleans_content_machine/social-intelligence/generated-posts/YYYY-MM-DD.md`

**All runs:** platform posts to `social-media/posts/<platform>/[month-year].md`, appending
to the monthly file if it exists.

## Step B — Publish only to live routes

Read `skills/coventry-cleans/publishing.md`. Post only to routes marked LIVE.

- **Facebook** — `FACEBOOK_CREATE_POST`, or `FACEBOOK_CREATE_PHOTO_POST` with an image. Page
  id `439312599272453`. Confirm a post id comes back
- **Instagram** — `INSTAGRAM_CREATE_MEDIA_CONTAINER` first, then
  `INSTAGRAM_POST_IG_USER_MEDIA_PUBLISH`. In that order. The image must be at a publicly
  reachable URL because Instagram fetches it
- **LinkedIn** — `LINKEDIN_CREATE_LINKED_IN_POST`. **State in the report whether it went to the
  personal profile or the company page** — the connection is Taka's personal profile, so
  confirm the destination before assuming company
- **Google Business** — POST to the live Make webhook with `platform` set to exactly
  `googlebusiness`, plus `title` and `caption`. The scenario replies with the body `accepted`;
  **that string is the confirmation.** A 200 without it means nothing posted
- **Nextdoor** — no route. Draft only

**Never treat a bare 200 as proof of publication.** Nine webhooks in the Make account are
enabled and attached to nothing; they return success and discard the payload. If the
response does not confirm the downstream module ran, report it as unconfirmed.
